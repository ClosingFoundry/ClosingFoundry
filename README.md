## What I build

**Closing OS** ([os.closingfoundry.com](https://os.closingfoundry.com), white-labelled as fac16)
A multi-tenant revenue platform with role-based access and client, partner and investor portals. It has 18 applications across Score, Design, Enable and Run, including:

- the Growth Score standard, Sales Capability Rubric and Sales Readiness Score
- Deal Inspection and Strawman value engineering
- an OS Configurator for CRM deployment packs
- a Seller Income Planning Workbook
- the Foundry Wiki IP engine

**Next-best-action data layer and AE copilot**
A data layer built from a sales playbook: a MEDDPICC question bank and 0–3 scoring rubric, stage behaviours with leading metrics, and stage playbooks from qualify to contract. It powers a full-cycle AE copilot: pre-discovery research, discovery and demo plans, business cases, proposals, negotiation plans and deal inspection from call transcripts.

**closingfoundry.com**
Moved off Webflow to Cloudflare Pages, with byte-for-byte parity checks, a Markdown CMS builder and serverless forms.
